Private, encrypted photo & video vault for iPhone

About

iVeç keeps your photos and videos private. Everything you add is encrypted on your iPhone with AES‑256 and protected by a PIN you choose, plus Face ID or Touch ID. The app is fully offline — no accounts, no cloud, no analytics — so your media never leaves your device.
